# The Iceberg Model - Quest for Entropy #13

The article **"The Iceberg Model"** (Quest for Entropy #13), archived as published,
with its assets. It introduces the model: a two-layer computational toy architecture
in which the classic mysteries of quantum mechanics and relativity read as properties
of a distributed system seen from inside.

The model itself - the paper, the code, and the full examination battery - lives in
its own repository: https://github.com/questforentropy/iceberg-model
(DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22046196). Reproduction instructions are there, not here.

## What is in here

- `article.md` - the piece as published
- `assets/` - the article's images
